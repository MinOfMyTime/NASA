# Validation

Percentile = share of valid land cells (0.05 deg) scoring lower than the site. `cell` = the 0.05 deg cell containing the site coordinate; `best 0.25 deg` = best cell within +-0.125 deg. The **development set** was used while building the method; the **blind set** was scored once with the frozen settings and is reported as-is. The **null test** shows 1,000 random valid land cells for comparison. Generated by `src/validate.py`.

## Blind-set references

- **Pilbara, North Pole Dome (Dresser Fm.)**: Van Kranendonk et al. 2008, Precambrian Res. 167, 93 (3.48 Ga hydrothermal/biosignature analog; Mars 2020 rehearsal site)
- **McMurdo Dry Valleys (Taylor Valley)**: Doran et al. 2002, JGR 107, 4772 (hyper-arid polar desert; outside the analysis latitude range -> expect n/a)
- **Lonar Crater**: Maloof et al. 2010, GSA Bull. 122, 109 (impact crater in basalt)
- **Dallol / Danakil**: Cavalazzi et al. 2019, Astrobiology 19, 553 (hydrothermal, hyper-saline, acidic)
- **Svalbard, Bockfjord volcanic complex (Sverrefjell)**: Treiman et al. 2002, EPSL 204, 323 (carbonate-bearing volcanics, AMASE expeditions)
- **Kilbourne Hole maar**: Hoffer 1976, NM Bur. Mines Circ. 149 (maar crater; NASA Desert RATS / astronaut geology training)
